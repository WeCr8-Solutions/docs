---
title: "Notifications and Alerts"
description: "Configure how and when Jobline.ai notifies you about jobs, machines, and team activity."
---

Jobline.ai has two separate notification systems:

- **Notifications** (Settings > Notifications) — your personal preferences for which events send you an in-app notification or email
- **Alerts** (Settings > Alerts) — org-level thresholds that trigger automatic alerts for supervisors and admins when production conditions exceed defined limits

## Personal notification settings

Go to **Settings > Notifications** to configure which events notify you.

You can enable or disable notifications for each of the following events, independently for in-app and email:

| Event | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| New handoff submitted for my team | A new shift handoff has been submitted at a station assigned to your team |
| Job state changed (blocker, on-hold) | A work order's status has been set to a blocked or held state |
| NCR assigned to me | A non-conformance report has been assigned to you for action |
| Issue assigned to me | An issue from a handoff or work order has been assigned to you |
| Machine alarm active | A station in your team has entered an alarm or e-stop state |

Toggle each event on or off using the switches on the Notifications tab.

<Tip>
  Supervisors: enable **Machine alarm active** notifications so you know immediately when a machine needs attention, even if you're not watching the dashboard.
</Tip>

## Smart alert thresholds

Smart alerts are org-level rules that notify supervisors and admins when production conditions go outside acceptable bounds. These are configured in **Settings > Alerts** and require Admin or Supervisor access to change.

| Alert | What triggers it |
|-------|-----------------|
| Job overdue | A work order's due date has passed and it has not been completed |
| Machine alarm | A station enters an alarm or e-stop state |
| High scrap rate | Scrap rate on a work order exceeds the percentage threshold you set |
| ERP sync failure | The ERP connector fails 3 consecutive sync attempts |

For each threshold, enter the value that makes sense for your shop — for example, a scrap rate threshold of 5% for tight-tolerance aerospace work, or a longer overdue window for jobs with flexible due dates.

## Email notifications

Email notifications are controlled from **Settings > Notifications**. Toggle each event type on or off for email. Notifications go to the email address associated with your Jobline.ai account.

If you are not receiving emails, check your spam or junk folder, and confirm your email address is correct in **Settings > General**.
