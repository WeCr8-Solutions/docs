description: "Configure your organization's name, shift schedule, manufacturing defaults, and billing."

Organization settings control the defaults for your entire shop. Changes you make here affect every member in your organization — so most of these tabs are restricted to Owner and Admin roles.

<Note>
  Only the Owner and Admin roles can change Organization, Manufacturing, Shifts, Work Centers, and Alert settings. Operators and Viewers will see a lock icon on those tabs.
</Note>

To access settings, click your name or the gear icon in the top navigation, then select **Settings**.

## Settings tabs

Jobline.ai organizes settings into the following tabs:

| Tab | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| General | Your personal display name, profile picture, and theme |
| Organization | Your org's name and industry type |
| Billing | Plan, seats, and invoices |
| Manufacturing | Work center types and part catalog |
| Shifts | Shift names and start/end times |
| Work Centers | Manage stations on your shop floor |
| Notifications | Personal alert preferences |
| Alerts | Org-level smart alert thresholds |
| Onboarding | Setup wizard and guided tour |
| ERP | ERP connector configuration (Enterprise plan) |
| Marketplace | Machine profiles and add-ons |

## General tab

The General tab controls your personal preferences. These settings apply to your account only and do not affect other members.

- **Display name** — the name other members see in handoffs and assignments
- **Profile picture** — upload a photo or avatar
- **Theme** — switch between light and dark mode

## Organization tab

The Organization tab sets your shop's identity in Jobline.ai. Only Owners and Admins can edit these fields.

- **Organization name** — the name displayed across the app and in reports
- **Industry type** — select the manufacturing category that best describes your shop (e.g., Aerospace, Defense, General Machining, Medical)

## Billing tab

The Billing tab is visible to the Owner only. Use it to:

- View your current subscription plan
- Upgrade or downgrade your plan
- Add or remove member seats
- View and download past invoices

## Manufacturing tab

The Manufacturing tab controls shop-level production defaults. Admin and Owner only.

- **Default work center types** — set which work center types are available when creating new stations
- **Part catalog** — manage reusable part definitions that can be applied to new work orders

## Shifts tab

The Shifts tab defines the shift schedule for your shop. Admin and Owner only.

Configure each shift with:
- **Shift name** — for example, Day, Swing, or Night
- **Start time** — when the shift begins
- **End time** — when the shift ends

Accurate shift times ensure that handoff forms display the correct outgoing and incoming shift on the dashboard.

## Notifications tab

The Notifications tab controls your personal notification preferences. Each member configures this independently.

Toggle on or off for each event:
- New handoff submitted for your team
- Job state changed (blocker, on-hold)
- NCR assigned to you
- Issue assigned to you
- Machine alarm active

You can receive notifications in-app, by email, or both.

## Alerts tab

The Alerts tab sets org-level smart alert thresholds. Admin and Owner only.

Smart alerts automatically notify supervisors when conditions exceed the thresholds you set:
- **Job overdue** — alert when a job's due date has passed and it is not complete
- **Machine alarm** — alert when a station enters an alarm or e-stop state
- **High scrap rate** — alert when the scrap rate on a work order exceeds a percentage you define
- **ERP sync failure** — alert when the ERP connector fails 3 consecutive syncs

## Onboarding tab

The Onboarding tab provides the setup wizard and guided tour for new organizations. Revisit this tab at any time to restart the tour or check setup progress.

## ERP tab

The ERP tab configures the connection to your ERP system (JobBoss Cloud, Epicor, or Plex). This tab requires an Enterprise plan. See [ERP Connector](/integrations/erp-connector) for full setup instructions.

## Marketplace tab

The Marketplace tab lets you browse and purchase verified machine profiles, tooling packages, and other add-ons. Machine profiles pre-fill manufacturer specifications for your CNC stations, which helps populate shift handoff forms correctly.
