Every Jobline.ai account starts with an organization. Your organization is the container for all your teams, stations, work orders, and handoff records.

## Create your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to app.jobline.ai and click Get Started Free">
    Visit [app.jobline.ai](https://app.jobline.ai) in your browser. You can also download the Windows desktop app if your shop floor terminals run Windows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email and create a password">
    Use a work email address. Your password must be at least 8 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email address">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from Jobline.ai and click the confirmation link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your organization name">
    This is the name your team will see throughout the app. Use your shop's real name (e.g., "Precision Parts Co." or "Acme Machine Shop").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your primary industry">
    Choose the option that best fits your shop: aerospace, defense, general manufacturing, or other. This helps configure relevant defaults.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your plan or start the free trial">
    All new accounts start with a 14-day free trial of the Team plan. You can change your plan at any time from **Settings > Billing**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After completing sign-up, you land on the **Settings > Organization** screen.

<Note>
  You are automatically assigned the Owner role. Only you can manage billing and subscription settings for your organization.
</Note>

## Configure your initial settings

Before inviting your team, take a few minutes to configure the basics.

### Organization name and logo

Go to **Settings > Organization** to update your organization name and upload your shop's logo. The logo appears on printed handoff reports.

### Shift schedule

Go to **Settings > Shifts** and configure your shift structure. Jobline.ai supports three standard shifts:

| Shift | Default start |
| ----- | ------------- |
| Day   | 6:00 AM       |
| Swing | 2:00 PM       |
| Night | 10:00 PM      |

Adjust the start times to match your actual schedule. Shift names and times appear on every handoff record.

### Manufacturing settings

Go to **Settings > Manufacturing** to set your default work center types and build out your part catalog. These defaults pre-populate new work orders and handoff forms, saving your operators time.
