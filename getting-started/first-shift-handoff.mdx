---
title: "First Shift Handoff"
description: "Run your first digital shift handoff and see how information flows between shifts."
---

A shift handoff is the core workflow in Jobline.ai. The outgoing operator fills out what happened during their shift; the incoming operator reads it and acknowledges before starting work. This page walks you through your first handoff end to end.

**Prerequisites:** at least one station set up, at least one work order in the queue, and at least two user accounts (one for the outgoing operator, one for the incoming operator).

## Create your first work order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Queue">
    Click **Queue** in the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Work Order">
    Look for the **+** button in the top-right corner of the queue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the work order details">
    Enter the work order number, part name, quantity, and due date. These fields appear on every handoff form and help operators understand what they're running.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the priority">
    Choose Low, Medium, High, or Critical. Priority affects how the job sorts in the queue and whether it triggers alerts on the supervisor dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The work order appears in your queue and is ready to be assigned to a station.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Run the shift handoff

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the handoff form for your station">
    As the outgoing operator, go to the dashboard and find your station card. Click **Start Handoff**, or open the work order from the queue and start the handoff from there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the work order and operation">
    Pick the work order you're handing off and the specific operation you worked on this shift.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the current job state">
    Choose the state that reflects where the job stands right now — for example, "Part Running", "Ready for Pickup", or "Waiting on QA". Job state tells the incoming operator exactly what they're walking into.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in machine condition">
    Report the coolant level, air pressure, chip condition, way lube, and whether there are any active alarms. If anything is not OK, add a note.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter quality status">
    Record the number of parts completed this shift, any scrap or rework, and whether critical dimensions were verified. Enter the timestamp of the last good part.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add notes and open issues">
    Use the notes field to tell the incoming operator anything they need to know. If there's an open issue that needs follow-up, add it in the Issues & Follow-Ups section with an owner and due date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the handoff">
    Click **Submit Handoff**. The outgoing operator signs off with their name and the current time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Incoming operator acknowledges">
    The incoming operator opens the handoff record, reads through it, and clicks **I Acknowledge** to sign in. The handoff is now complete and saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The handoff record is permanently saved and searchable. Anyone on your team can look up past handoffs for any station or work order.

<Tip>
  Your first handoff doesn't have to be perfect. The goal is to get your team into the habit — details improve over time.
</Tip>
