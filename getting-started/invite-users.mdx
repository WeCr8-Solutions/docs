title: "Invite Users"
description: "Add operators, supervisors, programmers, and other staff to your Jobline.ai organization."
---

Jobline.ai has two ways to bring people onto your team. The QR code method is faster for shop floor onboarding; email invites are better for people who aren't on the floor.

## Option 1: QR code invite (recommended for shop floor)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Teams > Invite Codes">
    Click **Teams & Members** in the sidebar, then select the **Invite Codes** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the team you want to invite people into">
    Each QR code is tied to a specific team. People who scan it are automatically added to that team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate Invite Code">
    A unique QR code is generated for that team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print it or show it on a screen">
    Print the QR code and post it at the machine, on the team's whiteboard, or at the shift start meeting point.
  </Step>
  <Step title="New users scan the QR code with their phone">
    They enter their name and create a password. No email confirmation is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They join automatically with the Member role">
    The new user is added to the team immediately and can start using Jobline.ai right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  QR code invites are the fastest way to onboard a shift full of operators. Generate one QR per team and post it on the wall.
</Note>

## Option 2: Email invite

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Teams > Members">
    Click **Teams & Members** in the sidebar, then select the **Members** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite Member">
    This opens the invite form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the person's email address">
    Use the email address they'll use to log in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select their organization role">
    Choose the role that matches their responsibilities. See the role guidance below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    They receive an email with a link to create their account and join your organization.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Role guidance

Assign roles based on what each person needs to do in the app:

| Person                          | Recommended role        |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Operators                       | Operator                |
| Supervisors / leads             | Supervisor              |
| CNC programmers                 | Operator                |
| Quality inspectors              | Operator (Quality team) |
| Shipping staff                  | Operator (Shipping team)|
| Shop owner / general manager    | Owner or Admin          |
| Read-only viewers (management)  | Viewer                  |

Supervisors can view the supervisor dashboard, org-wide KPIs, and attention items. Operators can create and submit handoffs, update work order status, and check in to stations. Viewers can read records but cannot create or edit anything.

## Plan limits

The number of users you can add depends on your plan:

| Plan       | Included users | Additional seats        |
| ---------- | -------------- | ----------------------- |
| Single     | 1              | Not available           |
| Team       | 4              | Not available           |
| Enterprise | 10             | $7.99 / additional seat |

To upgrade your plan, go to **Settings > Billing**.
