title: "Teams and Stations"
description: "Create teams and add work center stations to represent your shop floor in Jobline.ai."
---

**Teams** group people by department or shift. **Stations** represent the physical machines, benches, and workcenters on your floor. Together, they map your real shop into Jobline.ai so handoffs, queues, and dashboards show the right information to the right people.

## Setting up teams

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Teams in the main navigation">
    Click **Teams & Members** in the sidebar. You land on the **Teams** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Team and enter the team name">
    Use a name that makes sense to your operators — for example, "Day Shift CNC", "Quality Team", or "Night Shift".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a team description (optional)">
    Describe the team's function or the shift it covers. This is visible to all members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the team">
    The team is created. You can now invite members and assign stations to it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Setting up work centers and stations

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings > Work Centers">
    Click **Settings** in the sidebar, then select the **Work Centers** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Station">
    This opens the station creation form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the station name">
    Use the machine's real name — for example, "Haas VF-2 #1" or "Okuma LB3000 #2".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the work center type">
    Choose the type that matches this station. See the full list of supported types below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign to a team (optional)">
    You can assign the station to a team now or do it later. Unassigned stations still appear in the station list but won't show in a team's queue until assigned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the station">
    The station is now visible on your dashboard and available for handoffs, check-ins, and queue assignments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Name stations clearly and consistently — "Haas VF-2 #1" is better than "Machine 1". Operators will see this name on handoff forms and the dashboard.
</Tip>

## Supported work center types

Jobline.ai supports a wide range of work center types. Select the one that most accurately describes each station.

| Category        | Types                                                                                                        |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| CNC Machining   | CNC Mill, CNC Lathe, Grinding                                                                                |
| Cutting         | Water Jet, Band Saw, Punch Press                                                                             |
| Forming         | Press Brake                                                                                                  |
| Welding         | TIG Welding, MIG Welding, Electron Beam Welding                                                              |
| Finishing       | Hardware Installation, Deburr Station                                                                        |
| Quality         | Incoming Inspection, In-Process Inspection, First Article Inspection, Final Inspection, Outgoing Inspection, Calibration Lab |
| Engineering     | Quoting, Engineering Review, CAM Programming, Tooling Engineering                                            |
| Logistics       | Purchasing, Receiving, Shipping, Tool Crib, Material Handling, Material Staging                              |
| Assembly        | Assembly, Cell Assembly, Final Assembly, Sub-Assembly, Kitting                                               |
| Maintenance     | Maintenance Shop, Preventive Maintenance, Facilities                                                         |
| Operations      | Production Support, Production Planning, Production Control, 5S / Kaizen, Safety Walk, Training              |
