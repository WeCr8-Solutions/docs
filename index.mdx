## Get started in minutes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your organization">
    Sign up at [app.jobline.ai](https://app.jobline.ai) and set up your organization name and manufacturing settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add work centers and stations">
    Define the physical machines and workstations in your shop — CNC mills, lathes, inspection benches, shipping dock, and more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Send QR code invites or email links to operators, supervisors, programmers, and quality staff.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start tracking jobs">
    Create your first work order, assign it to a team, and run your first digital shift handoff.
  </Step>
</Steps>
