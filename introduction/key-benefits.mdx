---
title: "Key Benefits"
description: "Why manufacturing teams choose Jobline.ai over whiteboards, spreadsheets, and paper-based systems."
---

When your shop runs on paper, whiteboards, and verbal handoffs, critical information lives in people's heads — and walks out the door at the end of every shift. Teams that switch to Jobline.ai stop losing that information, gain visibility across the entire floor, and spend less time chasing down job status and more time making parts.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Zero information loss at shift change">
    Every shift ends with a structured digital handoff form. The outgoing operator documents the current job state, machine condition, quality results, open issues, and what the incoming operator needs to know — all in a consistent format tailored to the machine type (CNC mill, CNC lathe, water jet, welding, or generic station).

    Supervisors can review and sign off on handoffs before the outgoing operator leaves. Every handoff record is timestamped and stored as a permanent audit trail, so you can always go back and see what was documented and when.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Real-time job and station visibility">
    The dashboard shows every work order in your organization — its current state, priority, due date, and which operator is on it. Station cards update in real time so you know at a glance which machines are running, which are in setup, and which have an issue.

    The Shop Floor Display can be mounted on a TV or wall screen near the supervisor's desk, giving your entire team a live view of the floor without anyone having to open a laptop.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Team-based workflow routing">
    Work orders move through your shop in a defined sequence of operations. Each operation is owned by a team — Programming, Setup, Machining, Inspection, Shipping. When one team completes their operation, the job routes to the next team automatically.

    This means the right people see the right jobs at the right time. Quality doesn't see a job until it's ready for inspection. Shipping doesn't see a job until it's been released. There's no need to call across the shop floor or send a text message — the system handles the handoff.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Quality tracking built in">
    Quality status is captured on every shift handoff: last good part timestamp, parts completed, scrap count, and rework count. Inspection holds and releases are tracked in the system, so quality and production teams are always looking at the same information.

    Non-Conformance Reports (NCRs) can be created directly from a work order, with quantity tracking and quality metrics that feed into your reporting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="ERP integration">
    On the Enterprise plan, Jobline.ai connects bidirectionally with your ERP system. Work orders, part numbers, routing, and status updates sync automatically — no manual re-entry, no discrepancies between systems.

    Supported ERP systems: **JobBoss**, **Epicor**, and **Plex**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Works on any device">
    Jobline.ai runs in any modern web browser — desktop, laptop, tablet, or phone. If your operators prefer a dedicated Windows application, the desktop installer and portable EXE are available for Windows 10/11.

    The Shop Floor Display is designed to run full-screen on a wall-mounted TV or shop floor terminal, giving everyone on the floor visibility without requiring individual logins.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
