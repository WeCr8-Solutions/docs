Jobline.ai is a digital operations platform built specifically for CNC machine shops, aerospace, defense, and precision manufacturing teams. It replaces the clipboards, whiteboards, and paper forms that most shops still rely on with a real-time system that keeps every operator, supervisor, and department aligned — from the first shift of the day to the last.

At the core of Jobline.ai is the shift handoff: the structured handover of a machine, job, and its current state from one operator to the next. Instead of a scribbled sticky note or a verbal rundown in the parking lot, every handoff is documented digitally — machine condition, quality status, open issues, and what the incoming operator needs to do next. Beyond handoffs, Jobline.ai tracks work orders across your entire floor, routes jobs through operations, monitors station status in real time, and gives supervisors the visibility they need without walking the floor.

## The problem it solves

When a shift ends, critical information disappears. The outgoing operator knew the tool was on its last few parts. They knew the program had an offset quirk. They knew QA was waiting on a first article. Without a structured handoff, none of that reaches the incoming operator — and jobs stall, parts get scrapped, and supervisors spend their morning chasing down what happened overnight.

Jobline.ai closes that gap. Every job state, machine condition, quality result, and open issue is captured in a structured digital record before the outgoing operator leaves. The incoming operator starts their shift knowing exactly where things stand.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your organization">
    Sign up at [app.jobline.ai](https://app.jobline.ai) and create your organization. This is your shop's workspace — all your teams, stations, and work orders live here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up work centers and stations">
    Define the types of machines and processes in your shop (CNC Machining, Welding, Quality, Shipping, etc.), then add individual stations — each physical machine or workbench.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Generate QR code invites for each team. Operators scan to join — no IT ticket required. Assign roles: owner, admin, or member.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start tracking jobs and handoffs">
    Add work orders, assign them to stations and operations, and begin completing shift handoffs at the end of each shift. Your shop floor data starts building immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>
