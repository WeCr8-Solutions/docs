---
title: "Who It's For"
description: "Jobline.ai is built for every role on the production floor, from shop owners to operators."
---

Jobline.ai is designed for teams in aerospace, defense, and precision manufacturing — particularly CNC machine shops where jobs move through multiple operations across multiple shifts. Whether your shop runs 10 people or 200, every role on the floor benefits from having a single, shared view of job status, machine condition, and outstanding issues.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Shop Owners / Operations Managers" icon="building">
    Get a real-time view of your entire floor — active jobs, station status, and anything that needs your attention — without having to walk the floor or wait for a morning report.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Supervisors" icon="clipboard-check">
    Monitor all stations across your shift, review and sign off on shift handoffs, catch problems before they become downtime, and track your team's output against due dates.
  </Card>
  <Card title="CNC Programmers" icon="code">
    Track which jobs are waiting on programs, confirm that setups have the correct program loaded, and hand off CAM notes directly in the work order routing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Setup Machinists / Operators" icon="wrench">
    Know exactly what state a machine and job are in before you touch it. Complete your shift handoff in under 5 minutes and leave a clear record for the next operator.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Quality Inspectors" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Receive inspection holds directly from the floor, log first article results and NCR reports, and release jobs back to production — all without paper forms or phone calls.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Shipping & Logistics Staff" icon="truck">
    See which jobs are completed and ready for shipment, track what's on hold, and coordinate with production when jobs are running behind schedule.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Jobline.ai is designed to be usable by non-technical shop staff. No software background required.
</Note>
