Jobline.ai has two dashboard modes that adapt to your role: a **Supervisor Dashboard** for org-wide visibility and an **Operator Dashboard** focused on your station and active work order. A third display mode — the **Shop Floor Display** — is designed for wall-mounted screens and TVs on the production floor.

## Supervisor Dashboard

The Supervisor Dashboard is for shop owners, operations managers, and supervisors. It gives you an organization-wide view of what's happening across all your teams and stations.

**At-a-glance KPIs:**

| KPI | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Jobs in progress** | Count of work orders actively being worked on |
| **Stations running** | Count of stations with an active job and operator |
| **Jobs on hold** | Count of work orders paused for any reason |
| **Jobs completed today** | Count of work orders that finished all operations today |

**Attention items** appear prominently on the supervisor dashboard — these are jobs that need action right now:

- Jobs with blockers (waiting on QA, tooling, material, or maintenance)
- Jobs approaching or past their due date
- Jobs placed on a QA hold
- Machines in alarm or E-Stop

From the supervisor dashboard, you can drill into any station or work order, review pending shift handoffs, and sign off on handoffs that require supervisor approval.

## Operator Dashboard

The Operator Dashboard is for individual operators and setup machinists. It focuses on:

- **My station** — the station you're checked into, its current status, and machine condition
- **My active work order** — the job you're running, with part info, quantity, and operation details
- **Session tracking** — your check-in time and shift elapsed time

The operator dashboard is designed to be simple and fast. Operators can update job state, log a delay code, and start a shift handoff without navigating away.

## Shop Floor Display

The Shop Floor Display is a full-screen view designed to run on a wall-mounted TV, tablet, or shop floor terminal. It shows station cards for every machine in your shop — updated in real time — so everyone on the floor can see what's running and what needs attention.

**Configuration options:**

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| **Display mode** | Supervisor (org-wide, all stations) or Operator (team-specific view) |
| **Team filter** | Show only stations belonging to a specific team |
| **Refresh interval** | How often the display polls for updates |
| **Auto-rotate** | Cycle through multiple station groups automatically |
| **Dark mode** | High-contrast dark theme for bright shop floor environments |

<Tip>
  Mount a tablet or TV near the supervisor's desk showing the Shop Floor Display for instant visibility without opening a laptop.
</Tip>
