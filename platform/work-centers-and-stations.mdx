---
title: "Work Centers and Stations"
description: "Work centers group your machines by type. Stations represent individual machines or workbenches on the floor."
---

Jobline.ai organizes your shop floor into two levels: **work centers** and **stations**.

A **work center** is a category of machine or process. It defines the type of work being done — CNC Machining, Welding, Quality, Shipping, and so on. Work centers group your stations together and determine which handoff form fields appear when an operator completes a shift handoff.

A **station** is a specific physical machine or workbench in your shop. Stations live inside a work center. For example, the CNC Machining work center might contain stations like "Haas VF-2 #1", "Haas VF-2 #2", and "Mazak QTN-200 #1". Each station is tracked independently — it has its own status, active work order, and handoff history.

## Machine profiles

For CNC machines, you can attach a machine profile from the Jobline.ai marketplace. Machine profiles contain verified manufacturer specifications — control type, axis travel, spindle speed, tool capacity, and more — so you don't have to enter them by hand. Profiles are available for major CNC brands including Haas, Mazak, DMG Mori, and others.

<Tip>
  Give each station a clear, unique name like "Haas UMC-750 #1" so operators can identify it instantly on the shop floor display and in the handoff form.
</Tip>
