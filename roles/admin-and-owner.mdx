---
title: "Admin and Owner"
description: "Owners and admins manage the organization, teams, billing, and all platform settings."
---

Owners and admins are responsible for setting up and maintaining the Jobline.ai environment for your shop. They configure the org structure, manage who has access, and keep the platform running correctly.

<Warning>
  Only assign the Owner role to one person (typically the shop owner or IT admin). The Owner has full access to billing and subscription.
</Warning>

## Owner

The Owner has complete access to everything in the organization. The Owner is the only role that can:

- Manage the subscription and billing
- View and change payment methods and invoices
- Cancel or upgrade the plan

There is one Owner per organization. The Owner role can be transferred to another user if needed (for example, when ownership of the shop changes).

## Admin

Admins have full access to every org-level setting and every user management function — everything except billing. Use the Admin role for shop managers, IT contacts, or leads who need to configure the system but should not have billing access.

## What Owners and Admins can do

- Create and manage teams
- Add and remove organization members and set their roles
- Configure work centers and stations
- Set up shift schedules and manufacturing settings
- Configure the ERP connector (Enterprise plan)
- View org-wide dashboards and analytics
- Generate invite codes for new users to join the organization
- Access the admin panel — user list, issue queue, data health status

## What they cannot do

- Impersonate other users or act on their behalf
- Access data from other organizations
- Owners cannot be demoted by admins — only the owner can transfer ownership

## Admin panel

The admin panel is accessible from the main navigation for users with admin or owner access. It includes:

- **User list** — view all org members, their roles, and their last activity
- **Issue queue** — review all open issues and follow-up items across the org
- **Data health status** — verify that your organization's data isolation is correctly configured

## Inviting new users

To add someone to your organization:

1. Go to **Settings → Members** or the admin panel
2. Generate an invite code or send an invite link
3. The new user signs up with their email and enters the code to join your org
4. Assign their role after they join
