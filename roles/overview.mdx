---
title: "User Roles Overview"
description: "Jobline.ai uses role-based access to ensure each team member sees and can do what's relevant to their job."
---

Jobline.ai uses role-based access control at two levels: the **organization** level and the **team** level. Organization roles determine what a person can see and do across the entire org. Team roles control access within a specific team.

## Organization roles

| Role | Level | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | Org | Full access — manage settings, billing, all users and all data |
| **Admin** | Org | Manage org settings, users, teams, and work centers (cannot manage billing) |
| **Supervisor** | Org | Production oversight — view all jobs, sign off handoffs, manage the teams they supervise |
| **Operator** | Org | Create and update handoffs and job statuses for their assigned stations |
| **Viewer** | Org | Read-only — can view dashboards, jobs, and handoffs but cannot make changes |

## Team roles

Within each team, members can hold one of three roles:

- **Team Owner** — full control of the team, including adding and removing members and changing team settings
- **Team Admin** — can manage team members and settings but cannot delete the team
- **Team Member** — standard access to the team's stations and jobs

Team roles layer on top of organization roles. For example, an operator who is a team admin for the CNC team can manage CNC team membership but still cannot access billing or org-level settings.
