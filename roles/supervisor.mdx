Supervisors are responsible for keeping production moving. You have visibility into every job, every station, and every shift handoff across your org. Your job is to spot problems early, sign off on handoffs, and make sure nothing is stuck without a plan.

<Tip>
  Start each shift by reviewing the **Attention Items** panel on your supervisor dashboard — these are jobs that need your input right now.
</Tip>

## Day-to-day responsibilities

As a supervisor, your typical shift routine includes:

- Reviewing the supervisor dashboard at shift start to see the current floor status
- Checking the Attention Items panel for blockers, late jobs, and machines in alarm
- Reviewing and signing off on shift handoff records
- Resolving blockers by assigning ownership and escalating when needed
- Updating job priorities as the schedule changes
- Approving NCRs that need quality disposition

## What you can see

Supervisors have full visibility across the organization:

- All active, pending, and completed jobs in the work order queue
- All station statuses and which operators are checked in
- All shift handoff records for all stations and all shifts
- All team activities and assignments
- Downtime trends and quality metrics

## What supervisors can do

- View and review shift handoff records for any station or shift
- Sign off on handoffs as supervisor
- Update job priorities and statuses
- Create and assign issues and follow-up items
- Approve NCRs
- View downtime trends and quality metrics
- Access supervisor dashboard KPIs

## What supervisors cannot do

- Manage billing or subscription settings
- Invite new users to the organization
- Change org-level settings (unless the supervisor is also assigned the Admin role)

## Supervisor dashboard KPIs

The supervisor dashboard shows four key metrics at a glance:

| KPI | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Jobs in progress** | Active jobs currently running or in setup |
| **Stations running** | How many stations have operators checked in and jobs active |
| **Jobs on hold** | Jobs blocked, waiting, or paused |
| **Attention items** | Blockers, overdue jobs, and open issues requiring supervisor action |

The Attention Items panel is the most important part of your dashboard. It surfaces the jobs and issues that need your involvement — things that are late, stuck, or flagged as high/critical priority.
