title: "Access Control"
description: "How Jobline.ai ensures each user can only see and modify their own organization's data."
---

## Organization-level isolation

Every piece of data in Jobline.ai — work orders, handoff records, stations, teams, NCRs — is scoped to your organization. Users in other organizations cannot see or access your data, even if they use the same platform.

This isolation is enforced at the database level on every table. It cannot be bypassed by manipulating API requests.

## Authentication

Users authenticate with an email address and password. Sessions are managed via secure JWT tokens that expire after a period of inactivity, requiring re-login.

<Note>
  Multi-factor authentication (MFA) enforcement is planned for a future release. In the meantime, encourage your team to use strong, unique passwords.
</Note>

## Role-based access

What each user can do is determined by their organization role:

| Role | Access level |
|------|-------------|
| Owner | Full access including billing |
| Admin | Full access except billing |
| Supervisor | Production oversight — view all jobs, sign off handoffs |
| Operator | Create and update handoffs and job statuses for their stations |
| Viewer | Read-only — cannot create or modify any records |

Role checks are enforced on every API call, not just in the UI. An Operator cannot create admin actions even if they know the endpoint.

## Team-level scoping

Within an organization, teams further scope visibility. Operators assigned to a team primarily interact with that team's stations and queue. Supervisors, Admins, and Owners have org-wide visibility across all teams.

## Removing access

When a team member leaves your shop, remove them immediately:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Teams > Members">
    Find the member in the organization member list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the member">
    Click the menu next to their name and select **Remove from Organization**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Their session is invalidated and they can no longer access your organization's data.
  </Step>
</Steps>
