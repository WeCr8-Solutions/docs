---
title: "Best Practices"
description: "Recommended practices to keep your Jobline.ai organization secure."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Assign the minimum required role">
    Don't give everyone Admin access. Use **Operator** for shop floor staff, **Supervisor** for leads and shift managers, and **Admin** only for the person who needs to configure the system. Reserve **Owner** for the shop owner or IT administrator who manages billing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Use strong passwords">
    Jobline.ai does not enforce a password policy by default. Establish a shop standard: minimum 10 characters, no reuse of previous passwords. Avoid simple passwords like names or dates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Remove departing employees immediately">
    When someone leaves the shop, remove them from your organization the same day. Go to **Teams > Members**, find their name, and click **Remove from Organization**. Their session is revoked immediately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Use QR code invites for shop floor onboarding">
    QR code invites are faster and more controlled than email invites for operators. Each code can be deactivated after use. Avoid sharing login credentials between team members.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Review the activity log periodically">
    Admins should review the activity log monthly for unexpected login locations, unusual role changes, or admin actions they don't recognize. Go to the **Admin** panel to access the activity log.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Handle ITAR and export-controlled data carefully">
    If your shop handles ITAR-controlled or export-controlled technical data, do not store controlled drawings, specifications, or programs in the cloud version of Jobline.ai without the appropriate government authorizations. Contact [support@jobline.ai](mailto:support@jobline.ai) for the self-hosted deployment option.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Keep the desktop app updated">
    Check for new versions of the Windows desktop app periodically. New releases include security patches and stability improvements. Download the latest version from your account page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
