---
title: "Common Issues"
description: "Solutions to the most common setup and operational issues in Jobline.ai."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't see any jobs in the queue">
    - Confirm you are in the right organization (check the org name in the top-left corner).
    - Check that you are assigned to a team. If you have no team assignment, you may not see any queue items.
    - Check if queue filters are active — look for filter badges in the toolbar and clear them.
    - Ask your Admin to confirm your organization role is at least **Operator**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The station I need doesn't appear">
    - The station may not have been created yet. Ask your Admin to add it in **Settings > Work Centers**.
    - The station may be assigned to a different team. Ask your Admin to check or update the team assignment.
    - The station may be marked inactive. Ask your Admin to reactivate it in **Settings > Work Centers**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I submitted a handoff but the incoming operator can't see it">
    - Confirm the handoff status shows **Submitted** — a saved draft is not visible to other users.
    - Make sure the incoming operator is viewing the correct station.
    - Ask the incoming operator to refresh the page.
    - Verify the correct work order and operation were selected on the handoff form.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm getting a permission error when updating a job">
    - Your organization role may be **Viewer** — contact your Admin to change it to Operator.
    - You may be trying to edit a job that belongs to a station or team you are not assigned to.
    - Supervisor-only actions (like approving NCRs or signing off handoffs) are not available to Operator-level users.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An invited user didn't receive the email">
    - Ask them to check their spam or junk folder.
    - Verify the email address was entered correctly in **Teams > Members**.
    - Try generating a QR code invite instead (**Teams > Invite Codes**) — it bypasses email entirely.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The ERP sync is failing">
    - Go to **Settings > ERP** and click **Test Connection**. The error message will identify the problem.
    - Common causes: expired OAuth credentials, changed ERP API URL, firewall blocking outbound connections to the ERP.
    - After 3 consecutive failures, org Admins and Owners receive an automatic email alert.
    - Check the sync error log for specific records that failed — you can retry individual errors.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Machine status isn't updating in real time">
    - Live spindle data requires the JobLine relay agent to be installed and connected to your CNC control network. Contact [support@jobline.ai](mailto:support@jobline.ai) for relay setup.
    - Without the relay, machine status is updated manually through shift handoff forms and job state changes.
    - Check the relay connection indicator on the station card — a grey dot means the relay is offline.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A job is stuck and won't advance to the next operation">
    - Check if the current operation has been marked **Completed**. It must be set to completed before the next operation becomes active.
    - If the job is in **Waiting on QA** state, a QA team member must release the inspection hold.
    - Check for an open NCR on the work order — open NCRs block the job until dispositioned.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
