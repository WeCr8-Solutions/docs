title: "FAQ"
description: "Frequently asked questions about Jobline.ai."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there a mobile app?">
    Jobline.ai is a responsive web app that works on any device — phone, tablet, or shop floor terminal. No download required. Open [app.jobline.ai](https://app.jobline.ai) in your mobile browser and add it to your home screen for a native-like experience.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does a shift handoff take?">
    Most operators complete a digital handoff in 3–5 minutes. Paper or verbal handoffs typically take 15–30 minutes and frequently miss critical details like alarm codes, tool status, and quality notes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I customize the handoff form?">
    The handoff form automatically adapts to the work center type assigned to each station. CNC Mill stations show machine readiness fields (program loaded, tools installed, offsets set); Welding stations show gas/wire level fields; Water Jet stations show nozzle and abrasive level fields. Each form also includes free-text notes fields for any additional information.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can multiple operators share one station?">
    Yes. The outgoing operator creates a handoff, and the incoming operator acknowledges it. Multiple users can view a station's status simultaneously. Only one operator is checked in to a station at a time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my data if I cancel?">
    Your data is retained for 30 days after cancellation. During this window, you can export your work order history and handoff records. After 30 days, data may be permanently deleted.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Jobline.ai work offline?">
    The web app and desktop app both require an internet connection. An offline/airgapped mode is on the roadmap. For on-premises deployments with restricted internet access, contact [support@jobline.ai](mailto:support@jobline.ai).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I handle ITAR or export-controlled data?">
    If your shop handles ITAR-controlled technical data, contact [support@jobline.ai](mailto:support@jobline.ai) for the self-hosted deployment option before storing any controlled data. Do not store controlled drawings, specifications, or programs in the cloud version without appropriate government authorization.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I import existing work orders from our ERP?">
    Yes, two ways: (1) **Enterprise plan** — the ERP Connector syncs work orders automatically from JobBoss, Epicor, or Plex; (2) **Any plan** — use the bulk Excel upload to import work orders manually from a spreadsheet.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the user limits per plan?">
    | Plan | Users | Additional seats |
    |------|-------|-----------------|
    | Single | 1 | — |
    | Team | 4 | — |
    | Enterprise | 10+ | $7.99/month each |
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    Email [support@jobline.ai](mailto:support@jobline.ai) or use the **Report Issue** button inside the application (available in **Settings > My Issues**).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
