---
title: "Inspection Hold and Release"
description: "How to put a job on inspection hold and how QA releases it to continue production."
---

Inspection holds stop a job at a quality checkpoint until a qualified inspector reviews the parts and makes a disposition decision. Use holds for first article inspections (FAI), in-process checks, final inspection, or any time a quality concern comes up during production.

## Placing an inspection hold

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the work order">
    Find it in the queue or on your station dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the operation status to Waiting on QA">
    Click **Update Status** on the current operation and set it to **On Hold**. Then set the job state to **Waiting on QA** or **First Article in Process** if this is an FAI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a quality note">
    Describe what needs to be inspected, which dimensions to check, any concerns observed during the run, and the quantity of parts available for inspection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an NCR if required">
    If there's a known non-conformance, open the NCR form and fill in:
    - Non-conformance description
    - Affected quantity
    - Part number
    - Work order number
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign to the Quality team">
    Set the NCR owner or assignee to the Quality team or a specific inspector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The job appears in the Quality team's queue. The supervisor dashboard shows the hold in Attention Items.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## QA releasing the hold

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the NCR or inspection record">
    Go to the Quality team's queue and find the job on hold.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the non-conformance details">
    Read the operator's notes, the affected quantity, and any measurements or photos provided.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Perform the inspection">
    Measure parts, check against the print, and record your findings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the disposition">
    Choose the outcome for the non-conforming parts:

    | Disposition | What happens                                                                   |
    | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
    | Rework      | Parts are sent back for rework. Rework quantity is tracked on the work order.  |
    | Scrap       | Non-conforming parts are scrapped. Scrap count is logged.                      |
    | Use-As-Is   | Deviation is approved. Parts continue to the next operation as-is.             |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the NCR as resolved">
    Add your inspection notes, findings, and disposition decision. Close the NCR.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the job state to resume production">
    Set the operation back to **In Progress** if there are remaining parts to run, or **Completed** if the operation is done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Production is notified">
    The job's updated status is visible to the operator and supervisor immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  All NCR records and disposition decisions are logged with timestamps. You have a complete quality history for every work order, accessible any time.
</Note>
