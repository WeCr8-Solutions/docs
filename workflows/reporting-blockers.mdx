---
title: "Reporting a Blocker"
description: "How to flag a blocked job so supervisors and other teams can act on it quickly."
---

A blocker is anything that stops a job from progressing: waiting on raw material, missing tooling, a QA hold, a machine in alarm, or a program that isn't ready. Log it immediately — your supervisor and the teams responsible need the notification to take action.

## How to report a blocker

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the work order in the queue">
    Click the work order from your station dashboard or the queue view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Update Status on the current operation">
    Find the active operation in the routing and open the status update form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the status to the appropriate Waiting on... state">
    Choose the job state that describes what's blocking the job:

    | Job state              | Use when...                                             |
    | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
    | Waiting on Material    | Raw stock or hardware hasn't arrived yet.               |
    | Waiting on Tooling     | Required tools are missing, broken, or not ordered.     |
    | Waiting on QA          | Inspection is required before the job can continue.     |
    | Machine Down / Issue   | The machine is in alarm, broken, or needs maintenance.  |
    | On Hold                | General hold — use a delay code to explain why.         |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the delay code that matches the reason">
    Delay codes let your team filter and report on why jobs are delayed over time. Options include: Material, Tooling, QA, Programming, Maintenance, Setup, Operator, Inspection, Shipping, Unknown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a note explaining the blocker">
    Describe what's needed, who's responsible, and when you expect it to be resolved. Be specific — vague notes don't help.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an action item in Issues / Follow-Ups">
    Click **Add Issue** and fill in:
    - **Issue:** What is the problem?
    - **Action Required:** What needs to happen to unblock the job?
    - **Owner:** Who is responsible for resolving it?
    - **Priority:** Low / Medium / High / Critical
    - **Due By:** When do you need it resolved?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The job state updates immediately. The supervisor's dashboard shows this job in the **Attention Items** section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Machine down example

Here's how a real machine-down situation flows through Jobline.ai:

1. Machine goes into alarm mid-run.
2. Operator sets the job state to **Machine Down / Issue**, delay code: **Maintenance**.
3. Note added: "Haas VF-2 #1 threw Alarm 100 (Servo Motor Overload) at 3:14 PM. Machine is off. Maintenance notified."
4. Issue logged: Owner — maintenance lead, Due — today EOD.
5. Night shift arrives and sees the machine status and the open issue in the handoff before touching anything.

<Warning>
  Don't walk away from a blocked job without logging it. Other teams — QA, purchasing, maintenance — need the notification to take action. An undocumented blocker means nothing gets resolved until someone notices on their own.
</Warning>
