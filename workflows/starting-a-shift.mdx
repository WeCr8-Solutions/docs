---
title: "Starting a Shift"
description: "What to do at the start of every shift to orient yourself and pick up where the last operator left off."
---

The first few minutes of a shift set the tone for everything that follows. Use Jobline.ai to get oriented quickly so you're not starting blind.

## Operator checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Jobline.ai">
    Open the app on your station terminal, tablet, or phone. Use your email and password to log in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check in to your station">
    Find your station card on the dashboard and click **Check In**. This tells the system — and your supervisor — that you're at the machine and on the clock.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the last shift handoff">
    Open the handoff record for your station. Review the job state, machine condition, quality notes, and any open issues the previous operator left. Don't skip this — it's everything you need to know before touching the machine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Acknowledge the handoff">
    Click **I Acknowledge** to sign in. This logs your name and the time, completing the handoff record for the previous shift.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the work order queue">
    Check your team's queue for active work orders, priorities, and due dates. Know what's urgent before you start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the supervisor dashboard for alerts">
    If your role allows, scan the supervisor dashboard for any org-wide alerts, machines on hold, or jobs flagged for attention.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your machine matches the reported condition">
    Walk around the machine. Confirm the coolant level, check for alarms, and verify the setup matches what was reported. If anything doesn't match, update the handoff record and notify your supervisor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Begin work">
    You're oriented and signed in. Start running.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Supervisor checklist

Supervisors have additional responsibilities at shift start to make sure every station is ready to go.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the supervisor dashboard">
    Open the supervisor dashboard and focus on the **Attention Items** section. This shows any machines in alarm, jobs on hold, or issues flagged by the previous shift.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check for machines in alarm or jobs on hold">
    Address these first. A machine down at shift start can cascade into missed due dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review shift handoffs from the last 4 hours">
    Read through all handoff records submitted since the previous shift ended. Look for incomplete sign-offs, open issues without owners, and quality problems.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm each station is staffed and checked in">
    Make sure every station has an operator checked in. Unoccupied stations with active work orders should be reassigned or flagged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear blockers before the shift begins">
    If jobs are waiting on QA, tooling, material, or maintenance, contact the responsible party now. Don't let blockers sit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Post the Shop Floor Display URL on a monitor at the shift start meeting point so everyone can see station statuses at a glance.
</Tip>
