---
title: "Updating Operations"
description: "How to update a job's status and operation progress throughout your shift."
---

Keeping operation statuses current gives your supervisor real-time visibility and ensures the next person who picks up the job knows exactly where it stands. Update the status whenever something changes — don't save it all for the end-of-shift handoff.

## How to update an operation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the work order">
    Find the work order in the queue or click on it from your station dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the current operation in the routing">
    The routing shows every operation in order. The active operation is highlighted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Update Status on the operation">
    This opens the status update form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the new status">
    Choose the status that reflects what's actually happening: **In Progress**, **On Hold**, or **Completed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add notes if relevant">
    If you're putting a job on hold, explain why. If you're completing an operation, note anything the next person needs to know.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The update is saved and visible in real time to supervisors and anyone else with access to your team's queue.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Status transitions

Use these transitions to accurately reflect where the job is at any given moment:

| From         | To           | When to use                                                         |
| ------------ | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pending      | In Progress  | You start working on the operation.                                 |
| In Progress  | On Hold      | A blocker stops the job — select a delay code to explain why.       |
| On Hold      | In Progress  | The blocker is resolved and you're running again.                   |
| In Progress  | Completed    | The operation is done and the job is ready for the next step.       |

## Advancing to the next operation

When you mark an operation as Completed, the next operation in the routing automatically becomes active. If the next operation is at a different work center or assigned to a different team, it appears in that team's queue immediately.

For example: if you complete Op 20 (CNC Mill) and Op 30 is at the Deburr Station, the job will appear in the Deburr team's queue as soon as you save.

<Note>
  Completed operations are logged in the work order's audit history. You can always see who updated what and when.
</Note>
